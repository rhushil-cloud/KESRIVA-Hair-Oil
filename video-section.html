How To Use KESRIVA — Professional Scalp Routine

Watch how salon expert stylists inspect scalp condition, detect initial dandruff/dryness, and apply KESRIVA Herbal Hair Oil for maximum absorption.

4 Simple Steps for Best Results

1

Inspect Scalp & Partition Hair

Gently part hair in clean sections to expose scalp areas affected by dryness or dandruff.

2

Recommend & Apply KESRIVA Oil

Dispense 5–8 ml KESRIVA Herbal Oil directly onto partitions using nozzle applicator.

3

5–10 Min Circular Massage

Use soft fingertip pressure in circular movements to enhance blood flow and follicle penetration.

4

Leave for 1 Hour or Overnight

Allow natural herbs to work deeply before rinsing off with a mild chemical-free shampoo.
